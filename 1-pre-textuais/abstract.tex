This work presents the development of a web-based tool to support structural analysis and preliminary sizing of truss bridges built from wooden craft sticks, with an application focused on the Popsicle Stick Bridge Competition (CP3) at the Federal Rural University of the Semi-Arid Region. The solution interprets FTOOL files, reconstructs element geometry and connectivity, and uses the anaStruct library to obtain structural analysis results. Routines were implemented to verify members under tension and compression, considering strength, stability, and construction criteria to estimate the number of layers and commercial sticks required. The modules were integrated into an interface developed with Next.js, React, and TypeScript, while Python routines run directly in the browser through Pyodide. For a model representing one bridge face, with 10 nodes and 19 members, axial force values differed by no more than 0.001 N from those reported by FTOOL. Preliminary sizing yielded a total of 154 member layers and an estimate of 430 sticks for the analyzed face. The results demonstrate the integration of the computational stages; however, the material estimates remain dependent on modeling assumptions and adopted material properties.
